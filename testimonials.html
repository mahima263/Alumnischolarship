Stories & Voices

The young women whose IIT Kanpur journeys are being strengthened by this scholarship, and the alumni whose generosity makes it possible.

Stories of Scholarship Recipients

Arundhuti Roy

M.Sc. Physics

The scholarship was of great help in my journey towards pursuing a Ph.D. It helped me cover the flight tickets for joining my doctoral programme. Beyond its monetary value, the support has encouraged me to believe in myself and pursue higher education. Such initiatives inspire young women to follow their academic dreams with confidence and determination.

Gungun Jain

B.Tech. Civil Engineering

The scholarship brought much-needed financial relief to my family during a difficult period, when managing my educational expenses alongside my brother’s college fees had become challenging. It eased the burden on my father and gave me greater peace of mind to focus on academics and opportunities at IIT Kanpur. I hope to make the most of this support and eventually give back to others.

Johyna Els Thomas

M.Sc. Physics

The scholarship came at a crucial time when I needed it most. I remember checking my bank balance after paying my semester fees and wondering how I would manage the next month. At times, I had questioned my decision to pursue higher studies. This support reminded me to keep pushing forward, believe in my journey and never settle for less than I aspire to achieve.

Neha Biswas

M.Tech. Materials Science

The scholarship came at a time when I was preparing to begin a new chapter in Germany. It helped me purchase essentials, including winter clothes and groceries, and also allowed me to give some money to my family. Being able to support my parents made me feel truly blessed. This support has given me the confidence and strength to move forward with hope and courage.

Alka Rani Kerketta

M.Sc. Mathematics

This support allows me to focus more fully on my academic responsibilities and research goals. I am sincerely grateful for this opportunity, which encourages me to continue developing as a researcher.

Ananya Aggarwal

Master’s Student

It has allowed me to focus more comfortably on my studies without constantly worrying about finances. Being selected also feels like recognition of my hard work and has given me confidence that my efforts are moving in the right direction.

Firstly, I am very grateful to IIT Kanpur for initiating this wonderful ASGS scholarship for meritorious girl students. I feel happy to support bright girl students from financially weak families of my alma mater. “

It gives me immense satisfaction that we as Alumni are able to encourage needy Girl students to achieve their dreams of an IIT Education. AJ
Farooq Ahmed Khan M.Sc. (Integrated) | CHM | 1985
“

When I attended IITK during 1985-90, my four siblings were also attending schools/college. A scholarship from Andhra Pradesh covered about 40% of my expenses. My parents borrowed the rest.

As proud parents of two accomplished daughters, my wife and I are thrilled to contribute to this fund to support the current generation of young women students at IITK.

FA
Geetha Krishnamurthy Ph.D. | HSS | 1986
“

At IITK, I taught special English classes for students who came from villages and poor families and weren’t exposed to English, the medium of instruction at the institute. What was striking for me was that there wasn’t a single girl student in those classes. I realized that girls didn’t make it that far. Scholarships like this help fix that problem.

GK
Hari Hariharan (V. Hariharasubramanian) Ph.D. | CHM | 1979

In 1969 I had to take a bank loan for my living expenses to complete my M.Sc in IITK. So, helping deserving students is very dear to my heart. My wish is that no deserving student should ever reject IITK for financial reasons. If other alumni who have made it in USA can join me, my wish can be fulfilled.

HH
