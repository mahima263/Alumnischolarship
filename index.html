About the Scholarship

The Alumni Scholarship for Girl Students is the first collective scholarship initiative of the IIT Kanpur community, bringing alumni and well-wishers together to support deserving young women who face genuine financial challenges but fall outside existing government fee-benefit norms.

This initiative was born from a group of women alumni, at PARWA 2025, who wanted to give back to IIT Kanpur, an institution that had given them so much and shaped their journeys. Their shared desire to create opportunities for the next generation of women scholars led them to come together and establish this continuing scholarship.

Today, the initiative supports 20 girl students every year, creating a larger and sustained impact through collective giving.

Bridging the Gap

IIT Kanpur’s real infrastructure has always been its people, including the students who couldn’t otherwise afford to be here. As part of Greater Than Us and its strategic priority, Talent – The People Who Carry It Forward, the Alumni Scholarship for Girl Students removes the financial barrier so that talented young women can pursue their IIT Kanpur journey without financial stress.

  • Providing scholarships to meritorious girl students in B.Tech. and 2-year Master’s courses.
  • Supporting those who fall outside government support norms but still face genuine financial challenges.
  • Enabling young women to pursue academics without the burden of financial stress.
  • Promoting holistic growth and development alongside academic excellence.

Scholarship Eligibility & Selection

B.Tech. Students

  • Eligibility: Female students with annual family income between ₹8–15 lakhs.
  • Who can apply: All undergraduate students, including those enrolled in dual degree programmes.
  • 1st Year: Selection based on All India Rank (AIR).
  • 2nd Year onwards: Selection based on highest CPI.
  • Continuation: Recipients must maintain a minimum CPI of 7.0.

M.Sc. Students

  • Eligibility: Female students with annual family income up to ₹9 lakhs.
  • 1st Year: Selection based on JAM score, with preference given to higher scores.
  • 2nd Year: Selection based on highest CPI, with preference to previous beneficiaries maintaining a minimum CPI of 7.0.

Additional Criteria

  • Preference will be given to students not receiving any other institutional or government scholarship.
  • The 20 scholarships will be allocated between B.Tech. and M.Sc. students based on merit and the applicant pool in each academic session.

Stories of Scholarship Recipients

Every student has a story, a journey and aspirations for the future. Meet the young women whose IIT Kanpur journeys are being strengthened through this initiative.

Arundhuti Roy

M.Sc. Physics

The scholarship was of great help in my journey towards pursuing a Ph.D. It helped me cover the flight tickets for joining my doctoral programme. Beyond its monetary value, the support has encouraged me to believe in myself and pursue higher education. Such initiatives inspire young women to follow their academic dreams with confidence and determination.

Gungun Jain

B.Tech. Civil Engineering

The scholarship brought much-needed financial relief to my family during a difficult period, when managing my educational expenses alongside my brother’s college fees had become challenging. It eased the burden on my father and gave me greater peace of mind to focus on academics and opportunities at IIT Kanpur. I hope to make the most of this support and eventually give back to others.

Johyna Els Thomas

M.Sc. Physics

The scholarship came at a crucial time when I needed it most. I remember checking my bank balance after paying my semester fees and wondering how I would manage the next month. At times, I had questioned my decision to pursue higher studies. This support reminded me to keep pushing forward, believe in my journey and never settle for less than I aspire to achieve.

Neha Biswas

M.Tech. Materials Science

The scholarship came at a time when I was preparing to begin a new chapter in Germany. It helped me purchase essentials, including winter clothes and groceries, and also allowed me to give some money to my family. Being able to support my parents made me feel truly blessed. This support has given me the confidence and strength to move forward with hope and courage.

Voices of Generosity

The scholarship is powered by alumni who believe in giving back and creating opportunities for the next generation.

Farooq Ahmed Khan M.Sc. (Integrated) | CHM | 1985
“

When I attended IITK during 1985-90, my four siblings were also attending schools/college. A scholarship from Andhra Pradesh covered about 40% of my expenses. My parents borrowed the rest.

As proud parents of two accomplished daughters, my wife and I are thrilled to contribute to this fund to support the current generation of young women students at IITK.

FA
Geetha Krishnamurthy Ph.D. | HSS | 1986
“

At IITK, I taught special English classes for students who came from villages and poor families and weren’t exposed to English, the medium of instruction at the institute. What was striking for me was that there wasn’t a single girl student in those classes. I realized that girls didn’t make it that far. Scholarships like this help fix that problem.

GK
Hari Hariharan (V. Hariharasubramanian) Ph.D. | CHM | 1979
“

In 1969 I had to take a bank loan for my living expenses to complete my M.Sc in IITK. So, helping deserving students is very dear to my heart. My wish is that no deserving student should ever reject IITK for financial reasons. If other alumni who have made it in USA can join me, my wish can be fulfilled.

HH
Varun Mishra B.Tech.–M.Tech. (Dual) | CSE | 2011
“

Our society will prosper only when its women receive equal care and opportunities to grow in every walk of life.

Contributing to this scholarship is my way of showing gratitude to all the wonderful women in my life—my Maa, wife, sisters, and two lovely daughters. They truly make me a better version of myself.

VM
